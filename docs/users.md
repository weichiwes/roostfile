# Who Roostfile is for

These are working assumptions, drawn from how people commonly lose track of files. They have not been tested with real users yet, so treat them as the questions to check first.

## The moment that matters

People do not open a file tool to browse. They reach for it in the middle of something else, usually with someone waiting. Every decision below follows from that: be there at once, then get out of the way.

## People

**Lin, who sends files all day.** Works in operations or sales. Files arrive through WeChat and email and end up as `微信图片_20260312.jpg` or `新建文本文档 (3).txt`. The name holds no clue, so the words inside are the only way back.

**Chen, who works in versions.** A freelancer or designer with `合同终稿`, `合同终稿-最新` and `合同终稿-最新-改` in one folder. A client asks for the one from two rounds ago.

**Wen, who reads and writes.** A student or researcher with a folder of PDFs and screenshots of slides. While writing, Wen remembers a sentence but not where it came from, and wants to quote it rather than open the file.

**Fang, who keeps the family's papers.** Rarely uses a file tool, then urgently needs an ID photo, an insurance policy or a year of receipts for a claim. These are the most private files anyone owns.

## Moments

| Moment | Who | What they need | How it is answered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Can you send me that file again?" | Lin, Chen | Find it and paste it into the chat without leaving the chat | Quick search, then `Ctrl C` and paste |
| "I just saved that. Where did it go?" | Lin | See it appear, and act on it at once | The arrival bar |
| "It was in a screenshot" | Lin, Wen | Find a picture by the words in it | Reading text in images |
| "I need the words, not the file" | Wen | Copy a quote out of a PDF or screenshot | Copy text |
| "Which one is the final?" | Chen | See the newest at a glance | Version marks in results |
| "It is reimbursement day" | Fang, Lin | Gather scattered files into one place | Collections, with Add all |
| "Where do I even start?" | Everyone, once | See it work on their own files in a minute | Suggested folders, then a word from their own photos |

## What this rules out

- **Almost nothing stays on screen.** The arrival bar appears only when there is news. Roo is the one exception, because it has to be there to take a drop: it can be hidden, and neither appears during a full-screen game, film or presentation.
- **Nothing takes the keyboard uninvited.** A notice that interrupts typing costs more than it saves.
- **No guessing unless asked.** A wrong guess about Fang's documents is worse than no guess, so sorting by subject happens only for files someone hands to Roo, and it says what it did.
- **Nothing leaves the computer.** Fang's files are the reason this cannot be a cloud service.
